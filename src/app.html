Series

Emails that go out one after another, a few days apart, to everyone on a list, including people added to it later. Add steps any time: everyone gets them in order, even people who already finished. Use it for onboarding, nurture and upsell drips.

Reusable messages with {{variables}}. A template is sent to each address at most once, even if you upload them again.

Mailboxes

The Gmail and AWS SES accounts you send through. Each has one or more senders: the From name, address and Reply-To an email goes out with.

Connect Google mailbox
Google sign-in isn't set up. Add your Google client ID and secret in Settings (DEPLOY.md, Part 2 explains where to get them).

Mailbox groups

Several mailboxes that send as one, mainly for cold outreach. A send to a group goes out from whichever mailbox has a free slot, by each one's limits and warm-up.

Gmail reply detection

Reply categories

Every human reply is sorted into one of these by a low-cost model on OpenRouter. The category is sent with the reply webhook. stop always exists and adds the sender to do-not-send.

Do-not-send

Addresses that must not be emailed. Each entry has a scope: Everything blocks every mailbox, Outreach and newsletters still lets a conversation mailbox answer them, Newsletters only just stops newsletters. A STOP reply or unsubscribe gets the scope of the mailbox it came from; bounces and complaints block everything.
